# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

## Purpose

OB-Personal-Sync is a personal configuration of the Google Drive Sync plugin for Obsidian. It synchronizes the owner's personal vaults between local devices and the owner's Google Drive account.

## Google Account Access

The application requests the `drive.file` permission. This permits access to Google Drive files created by or explicitly authorized for this application.

Authorized operations may include listing, downloading, creating, updating, and deleting those files to maintain synchronization.

## Data Use and Storage

Notes, attachments, file metadata, and selected Obsidian configuration files are processed for synchronization. Copies are stored on the owner's devices and in the owner's Google Drive account.

The plugin stores authentication credentials in its configuration. Configuration files may also be included in Google Drive synchronization and local backups.

When configured with the owner's custom OAuth client ID and client secret, the plugin exchanges refresh tokens directly with Google's OAuth service.

## Disclosure

Vault contents are transferred between the owner's devices and Google Drive. This personal configuration does not use Google user data for advertising, sale, or AI model training.

Google processes data under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

This informational website does not request or receive vault contents or authentication credentials. Its hosting provider may process ordinary website access information under its own privacy policy.

## Retention and Removal

Stored files remain until the owner deletes the relevant copies. Local backups and cloud copies must be managed separately.

The owner can stop synchronization by disabling the plugin and revoke authorization through Google Account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete files already stored on devices or Google Drive.

## Google API Data

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email listed on this application's Google authorization screen.
